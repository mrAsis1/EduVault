# Release Scorecard

## Scoring

For each of the seven release-workflow steps:

- **2** — step fully completed and verified
- **1** — step completed but with a gap noted and accepted
- **0** — step skipped or gap unflagged

**Total score** = sum of step scores / (2 × number of applicable steps),
as a percentage.

## Interpretation

- **100%** — every step in `workflows/release.md` completed and
  verified.
- **70–99%** — minor, accepted gaps (e.g. announcement delayed a day);
  release stands.
- **Below 70%** — release process broke down; treat as a process
  incident, not just a note — revisit `workflows/release.md` adherence.
