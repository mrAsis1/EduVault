# Accessibility Scorecard

## Scoring

For each checklist section (Semantics and naming, Keyboard operability,
Custom components, State and errors):

- **2** — all applicable items pass
- **1** — issues found with a linked follow-up
- **0** — issues unresolved or unflagged

**Total score** = sum of section scores / (2 × number of applicable
sections), as a percentage.

## Interpretation

- **100%** — full keyboard and screen-reader operability confirmed.
- **70–99%** — minor gaps tracked; acceptable to merge with follow-ups.
- **Below 70%** — do not merge; core interactions are not usable by
  keyboard/assistive tech.
